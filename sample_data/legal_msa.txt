MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA)
DOCUMENT ID: MSA-CORP-2026-V4
EFFECTIVE DATE: October 1, 2026
PARTIES: Apex Cloud Systems, Inc. ("Vendor") and Meridian Global Enterprises LLC ("Client")

RECITALS
This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") governs the procurement and delivery of enterprise cloud infrastructure and artificial intelligence data processing services.

SECTION 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES & DELIVERABLES
1.1 Vendor shall deliver the cloud-hosted analytical pipelines and machine learning inference services described in each applicable Statement of Work ("SOW").
1.2 Any modifications to the project scope must be executed via a written Change Order signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

SECTION 2. TERM AND TERMINATION
2.1 Initial Term: This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and remains active for twenty-four (24) consecutive months.
2.2 Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement or any active SOW without cause by delivering thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party.
2.3 Termination for Material Breach: Either party may terminate immediately if the other party breaches any material term and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) calendar days of receiving written notice.

SECTION 3. FEES, BILLING & PAYMENT SCHEDULE
3.1 Professional and Platform Fees shall be billed monthly in arrears as set forth in Exhibit A.
3.2 Payment Terms: All invoices are strictly Net thirty (30) days from the invoice issuance date.
3.3 Late Payment Penalties: Delinquent balances accrue interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permissible by applicable statutory law.

SECTION 4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PROPRIETARY DATA
4.1 "Confidential Information" encompasses all proprietary source code, algorithmic architectures, customer records, and operational documentation disclosed hereunder.
4.2 Non-Disclosure Period: The obligations of non-disclosure and restricted use shall survive the expiration or termination of this Agreement for five (5) full calendar years.
4.3 Trade Secrets: Protection of bona fide trade secrets shall continue in perpetuity as long as the information qualifies as a trade secret under applicable statutory trade secret law.

SECTION 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & OWNERSHIP
5.1 Client Ownership: Client shall solely own all right, title, and interest in and to all Work Product, custom models, and derivative data produced exclusively for Client under this Agreement.
5.2 Pre-Existing Technology: Vendor retains exclusive ownership over all pre-existing software, core frameworks, model weights, and proprietary vector indexing algorithms developed prior to or outside of this Agreement.

SECTION 6. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & PERFORMANCE
6.1 System Availability: Vendor warrants monthly uptime availability of not less than ninety-nine and nine-tenths percent (99.9%), excluding scheduled routine maintenance windows.
6.2 Service Credits: If monthly availability drops below 99.9%, Client is entitled to a service fee credit equal to ten percent (10%) of the monthly recurring platform fee.

SECTION 7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES
7.1 Vendor represents and warrants that the services will be performed in a professional, workmanlike manner adhering to prevailing industry standards.
7.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH HEREIN, VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING THE IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

SECTION 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
8.1 AGGREGATE LIABILITY CAP: IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED TWO MILLION UNITED STATES DOLLARS ($2,000,000.00 USD).
8.2 CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES WAIVER: NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, PUNITIVE, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS, DATA LOSS, OR BUSINESS INTERRUPTION.

SECTION 9. INDEMNIFICATION
9.1 Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Client against any third-party claim alleging that the software or services infringe any valid patent, copyright, or trademark.

SECTION 10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
10.1 This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the substantive laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to conflicts of laws principles.
10.2 Dispute Resolution: Any unresolved dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be settled by binding confidential arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) in Wilmington, Delaware.
