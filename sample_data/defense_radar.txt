# CLASSIFIED / ITAR CONTROLLED TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION

SECTION 1: SYSTEM IDENTIFICATION AND OPERATIONAL FREQUENCIES
The Airborne Active Electronically Scanned Array (AESA) Radar Subsystem, designated "Spectre-9", operates across dual frequency bands:
1. Primary Search and Target Designation Band: X-Band spanning 8.50 GHz to 10.68 GHz with agility hops every 12 microseconds.
2. High-Resolution Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) Mapping Band: Ka-Band operating between 33.40 GHz and 36.00 GHz for sub-decimeter surface imaging.

SECTION 2: TRANSMIT/RECEIVE MODULE POWER AND COOLING
The AESA antenna aperture incorporates 1,536 Gallium Nitride (GaN) solid-state Transmit/Receive (T/R) modules.
Each T/R module generates a peak RF power output of 18.5 Watts at 35% power-added efficiency (PAE), yielding an aggregate aperture radiated peak power of 28.4 Kilowatts.
Thermal dissipation is managed exclusively via closed-loop polyalphaolefin (PAO) liquid coolant operating at an inlet temperature of -40°C to +55°C, with a maximum allowable pressure drop across the manifold of 28.5 psi.

SECTION 3: CLASSIFIED CRYPTOGRAPHIC ID AND DATA LINK
All radar track telemetry transmitted to surface ground control stations is encrypted using Type-1 Suite A cryptographic algorithms implemented in radiation-hardened ASIC hardware (Part Number: CRYPT-AV-902).
The cryptographic zeroization switch must purge all volatile key material within 15 milliseconds of activation or upon cockpit ejection initiation.
